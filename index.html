TOTP 2FA Generator

Generate a time-based one-time password in this tab, then scan the QR code with a real authenticator app (Google Authenticator, 1Password, or Aegis). The two codes should match. Nothing is sent to a server: a strict Content Security Policy sets connect-src 'none', and the QR code is drawn locally as SVG.

Secret and options

A–Z and 2–7. A new 160-bit secret is generated when you open the page.

Security notes

  • This is an educational, local tool. Everything stays in this browser tab.
  • Do not paste a production account secret into a shared or untrusted computer.
  • SHA-1 is the default because it is what most authenticator apps still expect. SHA-256 and SHA-512 are available, but not every app will honour them.
  • The live QR is generated in this page from the otpauth:// URI. No third-party QR API is used.
